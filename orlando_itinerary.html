Theme Park
Head straight to Epic Universe — Disney's newest rival and the hottest park in the country right now. Hit SUPER NINTENDO WORLD and How to Train Your Dragon Isle of Berk the moment you arrive. The Wizarding World of Harry Potter is a must. Stay till close for shorter wait times.

Disney World + Tour Day

Friday · Sales Tour at 11:45 AM

7:30 AM – 11:00 AM
🏰 Magic Kingdom — Morning Rush
Theme Park
Hit Magic Kingdom first thing. Your hotel is only 5–10 min away! Go early to get TRON Lightcycle Run, Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, and Peter Pan's Flight before lines explode. Must leave by 11 AM for your tour.
📍 Hotel → Magic Kingdom 🚗 Uber
⏰ Park opens at 9 AM (8 AM for hotel guests). Uber from hotel takes ~10 min. Lines are 50% shorter in the first 90 minutes. Set an alarm!
11:45 AM – 2:30 PM
🏢 Mandatory Sales Tour
Required
11272 Desforges Ave, Orlando FL 32836. This is a timeshare presentation — your trade-off for the deeply discounted hotel rate. Plan 2–3 hours. Be polite, stay firm if not interested in buying, and you're free to go once it's done.
📍 Directions to Tour 🚗 Uber
⚠️ Arrive on time — being late can forfeit your reservation perks. Bring a photo ID. Don't feel pressured to make any financial decisions on the spot.
3:00 PM – 9:00 PM
🌍 EPCOT Afternoon
Theme Park
After the tour, head to EPCOT — it's close by and open late. Catch Soarin' Across America (new limited experience!), Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind, and explore World Showcase's global food stalls for dinner.
📍 Tour → EPCOT 🚗 Uber
9:30 PM

Free Day + Prep to Leave

Saturday · Early flight May 31

9:00 AM
🌿 Lake Eola Park
FREE
Downtown Orlando's most beautiful park — circular walking path around a fountain, swan boats to rent (~$15), and great skyline shots. Easy, free, and a vibe totally different from the theme park chaos.
📍 Directions 🚗 Uber (~$18)
11:00 AM
🛍️ International Drive (I-Drive)
Free to Walk
Orlando's tourist strip. Walk into Dezerland Park — 800,000 sq ft indoor entertainment center with FREE admission. Pay only for what you want inside (karting $27, bowling $30/lane, etc). ICON Park is also free to stroll.
📍 Dezerland Directions 🚗 Uber
1:30 PM
🍔 Lunch on I-Drive
Food ~$15
Tons of affordable spots — Shake Shack, Yard House, or a quick stop at a local spot. Budget $12–22 for a solid meal.
3:00 PM – 8:00 PM
🏊 Hotel Pool + Pack Up
FREE (Hotel)
Hilton Buena Vista has a great pool. Take the afternoon to decompress. Pack your bags tonight so you're ready for an early morning departure. You did a LOT — you earned this rest.
⏰ Set your alarm for May 31 early. Request your Uber the night before if possible — use scheduled rides. Check out of the hotel the night before if your flight is super early.
7:00 PM
🍽️ Final Orlando Dinner
Food ~$20
Last night! Disney Springs is a quick Uber away and has great food options at all price points. Or grab something near the hotel on Buena Vista Dr. Don't stay out too late — early flight tomorrow!
📍 Disney Springs 🚗 Uber
