Disney World + Tour Day

Friday · Hollywood Studios + Sales Tour

7:30 AM – 11:00 AM
🎬 Hollywood Studios — Full Day
Theme Park
Head to Hollywood Studios first thing — go early for Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run (new Mandalorian mission launching May 22!), then Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance. The reimagined Rock 'n' Roller Coaster starring the Muppets just opened too. Leave by 11 AM for your sales tour, then come back after.
📍 Hotel → Hollywood Studios 🚗 Uber
⏰ Park opens at 9 AM (8 AM for hotel guests with Early Entry). Uber from hotel is ~10 min. Hit Rise of the Resistance and Millennium Falcon first — lines build fast. Come back after your tour for the afternoon/evening.
11:45 AM – 2:30 PM
🏢 Mandatory Sales Tour
Required
11272 Desforges Ave, Orlando FL 32836. This is a timeshare presentation — your trade-off for the deeply discounted hotel rate. Plan 2–3 hours. Be polite, stay firm if not interested in buying, and you're free to go once it's done.
📍 Directions to Tour 🚗 Uber
⚠️ Arrive on time — being late can forfeit your reservation perks. Bring a photo ID. Don't feel pressured to make any financial decisions on the spot.
9:30 AM — Snack
🌯 Ronto Wrap (Galaxy's Edge)
Must-Eat · ~$14
Ronto Roasters in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge. Roasted pork, grilled pork sausage, tangy slaw, and peppercorn sauce wrapped in warm pita. One of the most beloved snacks at any Disney park. Note: Ronto Roasters closes at 3 PM, so grab this early!
3:00 PM – 9:00 PM
🎬 Hollywood Studios — Return Visit
Theme Park
Head back to Hollywood Studios after the tour — your 1-day ticket is still valid. Catch everything you missed in the morning: Tower of Terror, Toy Story Land, Slinky Dog Dash, and the new Muppets Rock 'n' Roller Coaster. Stay for the nighttime Fantasmic! show.
📍 Tour → Hollywood Studios 🚗 Uber
4:00 PM — Lunch / Late Lunch
🎭 Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant
Iconic · ~$20–30
One of the most unique restaurants in all of Walt Disney World — you eat inside a fake 1950s drive-in theater in a convertible car while watching B-movie sci-fi clips. Order the Black & White milkshake and a burger. Make a reservation on the My Disney Experience app before your trip — this one fills up fast!
📱Book via My Disney Experience app now. If no reservation, show up at 4–5 PM and ask for walk-up availability.
7:00 PM — Snack
🍪 Carrot Cake Cookie (Trolley Car Café)
Legendary Snack · ~$6
Hollywood Studios' most iconic treat — a whoopie pie-style sandwich with soft carrot cake layers and cream cheese frosting. Find it at Trolley Car Café (the Starbucks on Hollywood Blvd). Also grab the Jack Jack Num Num cookie — a thick chocolate chip cookie that's crispy outside and gooey inside.
9:30 PM
✨ Disney Springs Evening
FREE
Literally a 5 min walk or short Uber from your hotel. No ticket needed. Live music, shops, amazing atmosphere. Great way to end the night without spending much. If you're still hungry, grab Steak Frites from The Daily Poutine — fries topped with flank steak, chimichurri, and bearnaise sauce (~$16). One of the best 2026 Disney Springs bites.
📍 Hotel → Disney Springs

Free Day + Prep to Leave

Saturday · Friend joins · Chill & scenic day

9:00 AM
🌿 Lake Eola Park
FREE
Downtown Orlando's most beautiful park — circular walking path around a fountain, swan boats to rent (~$15), and great skyline shots. Easy, free, and a vibe totally different from the theme park chaos.
📍 Directions 🚗 Uber (~$18)
11:00 AM
🛍️ International Drive (I-Drive)
Free to Walk
Orlando's tourist strip. Walk into Dezerland Park — 800,000 sq ft indoor entertainment center with FREE admission. Pay only for what you want inside (karting $27, bowling $30/lane, etc). ICON Park is also free to stroll.
📍 Dezerland Directions 🚗 Uber
10:00 AM — Breakfast
☕ Breakfast near Lake Eola
Food · ~$10–15
Grab breakfast before or after Lake Eola. The Stubborn Mule on Pine St is a local favorite for brunch. Or swing by a nearby Panera/Starbucks to keep costs low. You're in downtown Orlando — lots of walkable options.
1:00 PM — Lunch
🍕 Lunch on I-Drive
Food · ~$12–20
After Dezerland Park, you've got great options on I-Drive. Shake Shack (ICON Park) is solid and affordable. Yard House has great apps and a massive beer selection. Or hit Dezerland's food hall for quick cheap bites inside.
3:00 PM – 8:00 PM
🏊 Hotel Pool + Pack Up
FREE (Hotel)
Hilton Buena Vista has a great pool. Take the afternoon to decompress. Pack your bags tonight so you're ready for an early morning departure. You did a LOT — you earned this rest.
⏰ Set your alarm for May 31 early. Request your Uber the night before if possible — use scheduled rides. Check out of the hotel the night before if your flight is super early.
7:00 PM
🍽️ Final Orlando Dinner
Food ~$20
Last night! Disney Springs is a quick Uber away and has great food options at all price points. Or grab something near the hotel on Buena Vista Dr. Don't stay out too late — early flight tomorrow!
📍 Disney Springs 🚗 Uber
May 31 – Early AM
